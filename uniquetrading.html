About Unique Trading

Unique Trading is a marketplace where players buy, sell and trade in-game items safely. Every deal can be backed by a middleman, payments are held until the buyer confirms delivery, and every profile has vouches and a star rating so you can see who you are dealing with.

FAQ
How do I buy an item?
Open a listing and press "Contact Seller" (or "Make an Offer" for trades). The seller can then send you a payment request inside the chat.
How does payment work?
When you pay, the money is held by the site. The seller only receives it after you confirm that you received your items. The seller gets 90% of the price.
What is a middleman?
A middleman is a trusted member who holds the items during a trade so neither side can scam the other. A middleman is required when you trade across different games or with more than 4 Murder Mystery 2 items. Create a ticket on the Middlemen page to get one.
I did not receive my items. What now?
Press "No" when asked to confirm delivery, then press "Report". Support reviews the whole chat and the money stays on hold until it is resolved.
How do I report a listing?
Open the listing and press "Report item". Choose a reason, add details and send it. Our team will review it.
What does "Looking for specific trade" mean?
The seller picks the exact items they want in return and they are shown on the listing under "Looking for".
I was banned. Can I appeal?
Yes. When you log in while banned you can send an appeal. It will be reviewed by the admin.

Prevent scams
Use the payment flow in the chat
Pay only through the "Ask for payment" button inside the deal chat. The money is held until the buyer confirms they received the items. Never send money or items outside of the site.
Use a middleman for big trades
Create a ticket on the Middlemen page and let a middleman handle expensive trades. Check a middleman's rating and vouches first.
Never share your password or codes
Nobody from Unique Trading will ever ask for your password, email codes or Roblox login. Anyone asking for them is a scammer.
Check the person's profile
Look at their rating, how long they have been around and what they have listed before trading. Be careful with brand-new accounts and prices that are too good to be true.
Don't confirm delivery too early
Press "Yes, I received my items" only after the items are really in your inventory. Once confirmed, the money is released and can't be taken back.
Report scammers
If you did not receive your items, press "No" and then "Report". Support reviews the whole chat and the money stays on hold meanwhile.
