Prevent scams
Use the payment flow in the chat
Pay only through the "Ask for payment" button inside the deal chat. The money is held until the buyer confirms they received the items. Never send money or items outside of the site.
Use a middleman for big trades
Create a ticket on the Middlemen page and let a middleman handle expensive trades. Check a middleman's rating and vouches first.
Never share your password or codes
Nobody from Unique Trading will ever ask for your password, email codes or Roblox login. Anyone asking for them is a scammer.
Check the person's profile
Look at their rating, how long they have been around and what they have listed before trading. Be careful with brand-new accounts and prices that are too good to be true.
Don't confirm delivery too early
Press "Yes, I received my items" only after the items are really in your inventory. Once confirmed, the money is released and can't be taken back.
Report scammers
If you did not receive your items, press "No" and then "Report". Support reviews the whole chat and the money stays on hold meanwhile.
